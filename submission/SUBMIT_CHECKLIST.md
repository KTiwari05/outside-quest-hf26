# Week 1 submission checklist

Deadline: **October 12, 2026 at 12:29 PM IST**. Aim to publish by October 11 IST
to leave time to check the public links.

- Sign up on https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week1-2026-10-05.
- Generate one activity, save it, and try it outdoors. Record what actually happened:
  date, surroundings, steps that worked, anything awkward, and one observation.
  Add that account to the article. Do not invent an outdoor trial or personal feedback.
- Create the new public source repository within the entry period and push this
  project. Do not publish .venv, node_modules, .env, private documents, or raw agent logs.
- Upload the demo video to an accessible host. Replace the draft's demo and code
  placeholders with verified public links. Screenshots are also available.
- Open the official Week 1 Submission Template. Preserve its headings and the tags
  `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge`. The previous `weekendchallenge` tag is not required
  for this round.
- Replace/remove every square-bracketed drafting note in DEV_SUBMISSION.md. Preview
  the article and test the source and demo links while signed out.
- Publish one finished DEV entry before the deadline and verify its URL and tags.
  Only the first entry is considered if multiple are submitted.
- Check Hacktoberfest separately: DEV account linked, shipping address present, and
  submission activity credited. A local app or repository does not prove sticker credit.
- Record any post-deadline code commits in README.md as required by the challenge FAQ.
